Built for the operating table

A logger you can read from across the shack.

Type a call. UTC is captured. Band, frequency and mode carry forward. Lookups fill the rest while you are still talking.

◉

Giant callsign field

Readable from across the table so a logger sitting next to the operator can keep up without asking for repeats.

⌕

Instant lookups

QRZ.com, HamQTH or free FCC Callook. Name, location, grid and country fill in automatically. The station photo appears over the map.

⚠

Dupes flagged now

Duplicates are called out the moment you type the call — before you waste the QSO.

🌐

3D globe of arcs

Every contact is drawn as a colored great-circle arc. Filter by band to see just 20 m or 40 m.

▤

As many logs as you need

One per event, per day or per callsign. Change operator anytime. ADIF in and out for LoTW, QRZ, eQSL and everything else.

⌘

Your data stays yours

Plain JSON files on your machine. No account. No cloud. No subscription. Illustrated manual ships in the installer.

Rig control

The log and the radio stay in lockstep.

Connect almost any radio through Hamlib rigctld, flrig, or a direct serial CAT port. Frequency and mode follow the radio. Change them in the log and the radio tunes with you.

Operator-focused, not cluttered

Desktop carries the full station: logging, rig control, CW keyer and FT8 link. The phone app stays lean — log contacts in the field without dragging the shack with you.

CW & digital

Keyer, decoder, and FT8 in the same window as the log.

···−

Dedicated CW window

Full keyer with twelve programmable contest macros. Winkeyer, serial and rig-internal keying, plus a sidetone.

♫

Built-in CW decoder

Listens through the sound card and auto-fills the callsign and report it hears. RBN and CW Skimmer spots on your frequency fill the call before you finish copying it.

▦

FT8 / FT4 with WSJT-X

Decodes stream in. Call CQ and reply from the logger. Completed contacts land in the log automatically.
