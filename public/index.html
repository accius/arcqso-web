Great circle

Every QSO draws an arc.

Contacts land on an interactive 3D globe as colored paths from your station to theirs. Filter 20 m. Filter 40 m. Keep a log per event, per day, or per callsign. Change operator mid-log. ADIF in and out for LoTW, QRZ, eQSL and anyone else who still speaks ham.
